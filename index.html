What is real in our stories

Every rule has a source

The zombies and the aliens are made up. The survival science isn't. Every move in the stories is checked against codes, health agencies and engineering references, and each episode lists its sources. A few of our favorites:

01
Zombie Outbreak Ep 1

Backup power in 10 seconds

Hospital emergency power must come back within 10 seconds of a grid failure, under NFPA 110 and NFPA 99.

Source: NFPA 110 ↗
02
Alien Invasion Ep 1

Glass fools a heat camera

Window glass reflects the long wave infrared a heat camera sees, so it shows a mirror image, not what's behind the glass.

Source: Firehouse ↗
03
Alien Invasion Ep 1, Zombie Outbreak Ep 2

623 gallons per inch of rain

One inch of rain on 1,000 square feet of roof is about 623 gallons. Treat it before you drink it.

Source: USGS ↗
04
Alien Invasion Ep 1

Steel carries sound

Sound moves through steel at about 5,940 meters a second, about 17 times faster than through air.

Source: Engineering ToolBox ↗
05
Zombie Outbreak Ep 2 and Ep 3

The 28 day insulin clock

Out of the fridge most insulin labels give it 28 days, and insulin that has frozen must be thrown out.

Source: FDA ↗
06
Zombie Outbreak Ep 3

No flame indoors

A flame indoors makes carbon monoxide, which has no smell and no color. Never use a camp stove indoors or burn charcoal inside.

Source: CDC ↗
