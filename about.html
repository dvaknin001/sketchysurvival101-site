About Sketchy Survival 101

We make animated stick figure survival stories, told one day at a time, and we check every survival rule in them before it goes on screen.

On the channel, you're the one who's trapped. In Zombie Outbreak you're the night engineer of a 180 bed hospital when the dead take the city. In Alien Invasion you're a window washer 60 floors up when every light in the city goes dark. The characters are simple stick figures and the monsters are made up. The diesel math, the water, the medicine and the physics are real.

How we check the science

Every episode description ends with a section called What is real in this story. Each line is a survival fact from the script with a link to where it comes from, and the same sources are listed on our series pages so you can open them and check the numbers yourself. When a line in a script can't be traced to a solid source, it stays out of that list.

Our sourcing standard, in order of preference:

  • Peer reviewed research and conference papers
  • Government agencies: the CDC, FDA, EPA, Ready.gov, the National Weather Service, the U.S. Department of Energy and national labs
  • Codes and standards bodies such as the NFPA and the International Residential Code
  • University programs and extension services, such as the Florida Solar Energy Center
  • Original patents, manuals and primary historical records

Every figure we publish (a temperature, a gallon count, a time limit, a price) should link back to a source you can read for yourself. When we can only find a number in marketing copy or a trade blog, we say so, and we use the more conservative, better documented figure instead.
